# Note

A titled note on a lead or deal, or on its own.

## Information kept

- **Title** — the note's title
- **Content** — rich text, which may mention people
- **Pinned** — whether it is pinned to the top
- **Pinned by** — who pinned it
- **Pinned on** — when it was pinned
- **Linked to** — the lead or deal it belongs to
