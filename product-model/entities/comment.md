# Comment

An internal comment on a lead or deal, which may mention teammates and carry files.

## Information kept

- **Content** — rich text
- **Author** — who wrote it
- **Mentions** — the people it mentions
- **Attachments** — files attached to it
- **Linked to** — the lead or deal it is on
