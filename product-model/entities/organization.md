# Organization

A company or institution the business sells to.

## Information kept

- **Organization name** — its unique name
- **Website** — its website
- **Logo** — its logo
- **Industry** — its industry
- **Territory** — its sales territory
- **No. of employees** — its size band
- **Annual revenue** — its annual revenue
- **Currency** — the currency of its figures
- **Exchange rate** — the rate to the CRM's currency
- **Address** — its address
